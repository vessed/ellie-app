# Rulebook

## Rules for my little girl ♡

1. "Bingo" is our communication word and you may use it when you wanna talk about something important
2. Always talk to me about your feelings and if something happens you don't like
3. You must eat at least 2 meals a day (your body needs nutrition) and drink lots of water
4. Do something productive everyday, examples: cleaning up your room, baking something, going for a walk
5. Don't lie to me about anything, even if it's silly or you think its embarrassing, I can always tell when you aren't being truthful 
6. NO talking bad about yourself, I want you taking in positive thoughts about yourself
7. If you need reassurance about anything, talk to daddy
8. Always say I love you back to daddy if he says he loves you, even if you're mad or upset
9. If you're upset and want a break, tell me before you leave
10. Communication means very much to daddy, so always talk about your feelings
11. Even when you feel like the world is against you, I'm always for you
12. You must get at least 8 hours of sleep everyday
13. Ellie is always allowed to do anything she wants, just make sure you check in with me first
14. If I'm correcting a behavior of yours, I'm not mad. Sometimes daddy knows more than you do and he wants to help you
15. Silent treatment is never the option, you talk about what you want and need before you act on it
16. If you need something, come to me as your first option
17. Crying is always okay and puppy will never be shamed or put down for having emotions
18. Keep up on taking care of yourself, examples: cleaning your body once a day, brush your teeth at least once a day, if you do something dirty in clothes; change them, keep your room cleaned
19. Bedtimes for my baby
   - Weekdays - 2 AM
   - Weekends - 4 AM

## Reminders

- Rules can always be talked about and changed if puppy feels like something is unfair
- No is always an answer, no matter the situation
- I love you very much and I'll always be here for you
- You will always be my beautiful little girl
- Daddy loves when puppy takes baths and plays with her toys
